1. Introduction

AI Make Food ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website at aimakefood.com (the "Website"), our mobile applications for iOS and Android (the "Mobile Apps"), and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies, please do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Data Type Examples Required
Account Information Name, email address, password, profile picture Required
Profile Information Dietary preferences, allergies, skill level, household size Optional
Payment Information Credit card number, billing address (processed by Stripe) For Paid Plans
User Content Photos of ingredients, saved recipes, cooking notes, reviews Optional
Communications Support requests, feedback, survey responses Optional

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Data Type Description Purpose
Device Information Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers Service optimization, troubleshooting
Usage Data Features used, recipes viewed, cooking sessions, time spent Product improvement, personalization
Log Data IP address, browser type, access times, pages viewed Security, analytics
Location Data General location (city/region) based on IP address Regional recipe suggestions, compliance

2.3 Camera and Sensor Data

🎥 Important: When you use our ingredient scanning feature, your camera captures images of your kitchen and ingredients. Here's how we handle this data:

  • Processing: Images are processed by our AI to identify ingredients
  • Storage: Raw camera images are NOT stored on our servers by default
  • Results: Only the identified ingredient list is saved to your account
  • Optional: You may choose to save photos for your recipe history

2.4 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

  • Social Login Providers: If you sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook, we receive your name, email, and profile picture
  • Analytics Partners: Aggregated usage statistics
  • Payment Processors: Transaction confirmation (not full card details)

6. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

6.1 Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access Controls: Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
  • Infrastructure: Hosted on SOC 2 compliant cloud infrastructure
  • Monitoring: 24/7 security monitoring and intrusion detection
  • Auditing: Regular security audits and penetration testing

6.2 Organizational Safeguards

  • Employee security training
  • Limited data access on need-to-know basis
  • Incident response procedures
  • Regular security assessments

6.3 Your Responsibilities

Help us keep your account secure by:

  • Using a strong, unique password
  • Enabling two-factor authentication
  • Not sharing your login credentials
  • Logging out on shared devices
  • Reporting suspicious activity immediately
